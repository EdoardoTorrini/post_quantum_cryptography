# Error-Correcting Codes

Coding theory in its broadest sense deals with techniques for the:
  - efficient (data compression)
  - secure (encryption and authentication)
  - reliability (error-correction codes)
transmission of data over communications channels that might be subject to non-malicious errors (noise) and adversarial intrusion.
The latter includes passive intrusion (eavesdropping) and active intrusion (injection/deletion/modification).
